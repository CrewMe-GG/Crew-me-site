Getting started

Use Discord's slash commands by typing / in a server where Crew Me is installed.

Server administrators should begin with /crewme_setup to configure the management role, staff role, and time zone. Configure individual features before using them.

Use /help in Discord to open this guide privately.

Staff activity

  • /activity — View weekly staff activity. A member can optionally be specified.
  • /leaderboard — Post the current staff activity leaderboard.
  • /staff_profile — View a staff member's profile.
  • /log_event — Record activity credits for a staff event.
  • /voice_settings — Choose which voice channels count toward staff activity.
  • /monthly_report — View a staff member's monthly performance report.

Absences

  • /setup_absences — Configure the absence system and its channel.
  • /absences — View active staff absences.
  • /absence_history — View completed absences, optionally for a specific member.

Warnings and staff reviews

  • /warning_channel — Configure the channel for warning management.
  • /staff_warn — Create a disciplinary warning.
  • /staff_warnings — View a staff member's warning history.
  • /staff_warning_notify — Send a saved warning to the staff member by direct message.
  • /staff_warning_revoke — Revoke a warning while preserving its audit record.
  • /demotion_reviews — View pending staff activity reviews.

Pending activity reviews are handled through review embeds with Reviewed and Dismissed buttons. Review decisions may require a reason.

Meetings and attendance

  • /meeting_create — Schedule a meeting with a voice channel and announcement channel.
  • /meetings — List upcoming meetings.
  • /meeting_attendance — View attendance for a meeting.
  • /meeting_add_attendee — Manually record a staff member's attendance.
  • /meeting_remove_attendee — Remove an attendance record.
  • /meeting_cancel — Cancel a scheduled meeting.

Meeting creation requires a title, start time, duration, voice channel, and announcement channel. Use Discord's command prompts to enter the required values.

Private staff discussions

  • /staff_ticket_setup — Configure the private ticket category and management log channel.
  • /staff_ticket — Open a management-initiated staff discussion.
  • /staff_ticket_close — Close and lock a staff discussion ticket.
  • /staff_ticket_add — Give a participant access to a staff ticket.
  • /staff_ticket_remove — Remove a participant's ticket access.
  • /staff_ticket_history — Review staff ticket history.

Server configuration and appearance

  • /crewme_setup — Configure management and staff roles and the server time zone.
  • /set_embed_color — Set the color used in Crew Me embeds.
  • /set_server_avatar — Set Crew Me's server-specific profile picture.
  • /reset_appearance — Reset the configured color and server-specific avatar.
  • /staff_dashboard — View an overview of staff activity and management records.
  • /crewme_info — View public information about Crew Me.

Permissions and troubleshooting

Some commands are restricted to server administrators or authorized management roles. Others require a feature to be configured first. The command may not be available if your Discord role lacks permission or the feature has not been set up.

If a command fails, check the required options, the configured channels and roles, and Crew Me's Discord permissions. Contact your server management team for access questions.
