Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Crew Me ("we," "us," or "the Bot"), an independent Discord staff-management project operated by its creator, processes information when Discord communities use the Bot.

1. Information we process

The information processed depends on which Crew Me features a server uses. It may include:

  • Discord identifiers: user, server (guild), channel, role, and message identifiers needed for Bot functions and configuration.
  • Staff activity: weekly message counts, eligible voice-channel time, activity credits, and active-day records.
  • Staff membership and evaluations: membership dates, weekly evaluation results, activity requirements, consecutive failures, and review status.
  • Absences: absence dates, return dates, reasons entered by users, status, and records of when an absence was ended and by whom.
  • Warnings and management reviews: warning levels and reasons, the identifiers of the people involved, notification and revocation details, review outcomes, decision reasons, and timestamps.
  • Meetings: meeting titles, start and end times, relevant channel and message identifiers, RSVP responses, and attendance durations.
  • Staff tickets: ticket topics, participant identifiers, status, timestamps, and links to related warning records.
  • Configuration: management and staff roles, selected channels, time zone, eligible voice channels, appearance settings, and other server-specific settings.

2. Message content and Discord communications

For routine staff activity counting, Crew Me counts qualifying messages from staff members. The activity database stores counts and related statistics, not the text of ordinary messages as activity records.

Users and administrators may enter text into fields such as absence reasons, warning reasons, review explanations, and ticket topics. Ticket conversations themselves take place in Discord channels and are subject to Discord's handling of messages and the permissions configured for those channels. The Bot's ticket database stores ticket metadata; it does not contain a separate transcript table in the current database schema.

3. Why information is used

Information is used to provide the Bot's requested functions, calculate staff activity, record meeting attendance, manage absences and warnings, support management reviews, maintain server settings, send notifications, troubleshoot issues, and protect the service from misuse.

4. Access and disclosure

Access depends on the feature, Bot permissions, database access, and Discord channel permissions. Authorized server managers may access staff records through management functions. Staff may be able to view their own records or information made available by their server. The project operator may access the Bot database and server infrastructure to maintain and troubleshoot the service.

Discord processes account information and communications under its own policies. Information may also be processed by infrastructure providers used to host the website or operate the Bot. We do not sell personal information as part of operating this project.

5. Storage and security

The Bot stores structured staff-management records in an SQLite database on the server where the Bot is deployed. The public website is hosted through GitHub Pages. The specific Bot hosting provider, hosting region, and any infrastructure backup arrangements have not been independently documented in this policy; contact us for current information.

Access should be limited to people who need it to operate the service. No online service or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. Do not submit passwords, payment-card details, government identification, or unnecessary sensitive information through Crew Me.

6. Retention

Crew Me does not currently have a documented, general automatic deletion schedule for all staff-management records. Records may remain in the database until they are manually deleted, removed by a feature-specific action, or otherwise removed through maintenance. Some actions remove specific records, while other actions preserve historical records for accountability.

We do not promise a fixed retention period for activity history, absences, warnings, meetings, tickets, or review records. The time required to complete a deletion request may depend on the record, technical constraints, and any applicable legal or legitimate recordkeeping needs. Backup retention, if backups exist, may differ from the live database.

7. Privacy requests and choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of information associated with your Discord account by emailing support@crewmegg.com. Include your Discord user ID and the relevant server or feature so we can identify the records. Do not send your password or identity documents.

We may need to verify that a request relates to you or that you are authorized to make it. Some records may be retained where required by law, necessary to address security or abuse, or needed to preserve legitimate administrative records. We will explain any applicable limitation. Server administrators may also need to remove information from Discord channels they control.

Server administrators decide which features to enable and are responsible for explaining their local staff-management practices to affected members.

8. Children

Crew Me is not intended to collect unnecessary personal information from children. Users must meet Discord's applicable age requirements and any additional legal requirements that apply to them. Server operators must follow applicable rules when deciding who may participate in their communities.

9. International processing

Information may be processed in the locations where Discord, GitHub Pages, and the Bot's hosting infrastructure operate. The Bot hosting region has not been confirmed in this policy. Data-protection rights may vary depending on your location and applicable law.

10. Changes to this Policy

This Policy may be updated when Crew Me's features or data practices change. The effective date will be revised when a new version is published. Material changes may be announced through the website or an official Crew Me announcement channel.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact support@crewmegg.com.
