Getting started

Set up your crew.

Welcome to Crew Me! Follow this guide to configure your Discord server, organize staff management, and get your team ready to go.

Before you begin

  • Crew Me is installed in your Discord server and can access the channels where it needs to work.
  • You have administrator permissions or the appropriate authority to configure the Bot.
  • Your staff and management roles already exist.
  • You know the time zone your server will use for staff tracking and scheduling.

Important: Keep management permissions limited to trusted people. Do not give the Bot Administrator permission unless it is genuinely necessary; configure only the permissions its features require.

Step 1 — Configure your server

Start with the core configuration command:

/crewme_setup

Choose your management role, staff role, and server time zone when prompted.

  • Management role: People who are authorized to manage staff records and use management features.
  • Staff role: Members whose activity Crew Me should track as staff.
  • Time zone: The time zone used for relevant dates, weekly activity, and scheduling.

Use the actual time zone offered by the command rather than guessing. If the configuration command is unavailable, check your server permissions and confirm that Crew Me is online.

Step 2 — Set up absences

Use:

/setup_absences

Select the channel for private absence threads and the role allowed to manage absences. Crew Me will post an absence panel in the selected channel.

Check that Crew Me has permission to view the channel, send messages, create private threads, and manage threads as needed. Authorized managers must also have the access required to view and manage those threads.

Staff can use the panel to submit absence dates and a reason. Absences are notifications, not approval requests.

Step 3 — Configure voice activity

Use:

/voice_settings

Choose which voice channels qualify for staff activity credits. Review the channel list carefully so that only the intended channels count.

Staff voice time is tracked only in eligible channels according to the Bot's tracking rules. Test a short session and check the results with /activity.

Step 4 — Configure warnings

Use:

/warning_channel

Select the private management channel used for warning records and management actions. Check the channel permissions so staff members cannot access private records unless they are meant to.

Authorized managers can use /staff_warn to create a warning and /staff_warnings to view a member's disciplinary history. A saved warning does not automatically mean the member has been notified.

Step 5 — Configure private staff tickets

Use:

/staff_ticket_setup

Choose the category for private staff discussion channels and the management log channel.

Review the category's permissions before opening a ticket. Only the intended participants and authorized management should have access. Test the workflow with a trusted staff account before using it for sensitive discussions.

Step 6 — Start using meetings

Use /meeting_create to schedule a meeting. You'll be asked for a title, start time, duration, voice channel, and announcement channel.

Staff can respond to meeting invitations, and eligible voice attendance can be recorded. Management can use the meeting and attendance commands to review records or correct attendance when necessary.

Check the Bot's permissions in both the announcement channel and the meeting voice channel before scheduling your first meeting.

Step 7 — Understand staff activity

Once your staff role and voice settings are configured, these commands are useful:

  • /activity — View weekly activity.
  • /leaderboard — Post the current staff activity leaderboard.
  • /staff_profile — View a staff member's profile.
  • /monthly_report — Review monthly staff performance.
  • /staff_dashboard — View the management overview.
  • /demotion_reviews — View pending staff activity reviews.

Activity requirements and review outcomes should be reviewed by management. Do not treat an automated activity result as an automatic disciplinary decision.

Step 8 — Customize your server

  • /set_embed_color — Customize the color of Crew Me embeds.
  • /set_server_avatar — Set a server-specific Bot profile picture.
  • /reset_appearance — Reset appearance settings.

Use /crewme_info to view public information about the Bot and /help to open the command guide.

Final checklist

  • Core staff and management roles are configured.
  • The server time zone is correct.
  • The absence panel is posted in the intended channel.
  • Eligible voice channels are configured and activity tracking has been tested.
  • Warning and ticket channels have appropriate access restrictions.
  • A test meeting can be scheduled and its attendance reviewed.
  • Managers know how to access the dashboard and pending reviews.
  • Staff have been informed about activity tracking and staff record handling.
